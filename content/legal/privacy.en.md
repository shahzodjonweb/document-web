## 2. What data we process

- **Account data.** With Telegram sign-in: your Telegram user ID, name, username and, if available, profile photo. With email sign-in: your email address and a password hash (we never store the password itself). With Google sign-in: your Google account ID, name and email address. Also: interface language, time zone, theme and plan.
- **Files.** The files you upload and the results we make from them.
- **AI data.** The requests you write for AI documents, slides and CVs, the files you attach to them, the outlines and the results. A CV request usually contains personal data about you (name, contacts, work and education history).
- **Payment data.** The receipts you upload, and records of payments: amount, date, plan, status and our decision.
- **Invite data.** Your invite link, who joined through it and the rewards earned.
- **Support messages** you send us.
- **Technical data.** IP address, browser and device type, session identifiers, and logs of requests and tasks needed to run and protect the service.

## 3. Purposes

We use personal data only to:

- create and run your account and sign you in;
- carry out the tasks you start and deliver the results;
- count weekly limits and check the Telegram channel requirement on the Free and Plus plans;
- check payments and switch plans on, and handle refunds;
- review the quality of AI results and prevent misuse;
- run invite rewards;
- answer support requests;
- protect the service from abuse and bots (including Cloudflare Turnstile);
- meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Legal bases

We process personal data on these grounds:

- your consent, given when you sign in and accept the Public offer;
- performance of the contract with you (the Public offer);
- our legal obligations (for example, accounting and responding to lawful requests from state bodies).

## 11. Cookies and local storage

We use only the storage the service needs to work:

- **session cookie** to keep you signed in;
- **CSRF cookie** to protect forms from forged requests;
- **language** you chose;
- **theme** (light or dark).

Cloudflare Turnstile, our anti-bot check, may set its own strictly necessary data to tell people from bots.

We use no analytics, no advertising trackers and no third-party marketing cookies. Because all storage is strictly necessary, we do not show a consent banner.
