This public offer (the "Offer") is made by **ARCANIUM TECHNOLOGIES LLC** ("MCHJ"), STIR 313458514, registered at Toshkent shahri, Yunusobod tumani, Qashqar MFY, Markaz 4 mavzesi, 25-uy (Tashkent, Uzbekistan) (the "Provider"). It is addressed to any person who uses the DocMaster service (the "User").

In this Offer:

- **Service** means DocMaster (formerly PDF Master): the website docmaster.live, the web app, the Telegram bot and the Telegram Mini App.
- **Account** means the User's personal account in the Service.
- **Plan** means one of the plans published on the Pricing page: Free, Plus or Premium.
- **Task** means one run of a file tool, an AI generation or an AI edit.
- **Period** means a paid Plan's 30-day term.

This Offer also serves as the terms of use. There is no separate terms document.

## 2. Subject

The Provider gives the User access to the Service, and the User uses it under this Offer. The Service includes:

- file tools for PDF and office files (merge, split, compress, convert, OCR, passwords and others);
- a PDF editor on the web and in the Telegram Mini App;
- AI generation of documents, slide decks and CVs, and AI edits of the results.

The Service is delivered online only. Nothing is shipped physically.

## 3. Acceptance

The User accepts this Offer in full by any of these actions: registering or signing in to the Service (on the web, in the bot or in the Mini App), or paying for a Plan. From that moment the contract between the Provider and the User is concluded on the terms of this Offer.

If the User does not agree with the Offer, they must not use the Service.

## 4. Account and sign-in

The User signs in with Telegram, with an email address and password, or with Google. One Account works across the website, the bot and the Mini App, with the same Plan and the same weekly limits.

The User keeps their sign-in details safe and is responsible for actions taken through their Account. The User tells support at once if they believe someone else has access to it.

## 5. Plans and prices

The Plans, their weekly limits and their prices are published on the Pricing page (docmaster.live/en/pricing) and are part of this Offer. Every feature is available on every Plan; Plans differ in weekly limits, AI edits per file and the Telegram channel requirement.

- A week starts on Monday at 00:00 Tashkent time for every User.
- A Task counts once it is started. A Task that fails, is cancelled, expires or produces nothing is not counted.
- On the Free and Plus Plans the User must join the Provider's Telegram channel(s) listed in the Service before starting a Task. Premium has no such requirement.

Prices are in Uzbek so'm (so‘m). The Provider is not a VAT payer: prices are final and VAT is not charged. The Provider may set a temporary discounted price with a last day; the price shown at the moment of payment applies.

## 6. Payment

Paid Plans are paid today by bank-card transfer from a Uzcard, Humo, Visa or Mastercard card, to the details shown in the Service. After paying, the User uploads the payment receipt in the Service.

The Provider's staff check the receipt. The Provider does not promise a fixed checking time. If the receipt cannot be matched to a payment, the Provider may ask for more information or decline it.

Other payment methods may be added later; when they are, this Offer and the Pricing page will describe them.

## 7. Activation and delivery

The Service is delivered electronically. A paid Plan starts when the Provider approves the payment, and the Period runs 30 days from that moment. The User sees the active Plan and its end date in the Account.

The Service counts as delivered from activation.

## 8. Term and no auto-renewal

This Offer applies for as long as the User uses the Service. A paid Plan lasts one Period of 30 days and is not renewed automatically. To continue, the User pays again for the next 30 days. When a Period ends without a new payment, the Account returns to the Free Plan.

Plus months earned through invite rewards are Plus Periods at no charge. They are not money, cannot be exchanged for money and are not refunded.

## 9. Refunds

A full refund is given if the User asks within 3 days of activation and has not run any Task on the paid Plan. Duplicate payments and payments for a Plan that was never activated are always refunded. Money goes back to the same card within 10 working days.

The full rules are in the [Refund policy](/en/legal/refunds), which is part of this Offer.

## 10. User obligations and acceptable use

The User agrees to:

- upload and create only content they have the right to use, and not infringe anyone's copyright, trademarks or personal data;
- not use the Service for illegal content or purposes under the laws of Uzbekistan;
- not try to bypass weekly limits, the channel requirement or anti-bot checks, and not open several Accounts to get more limits;
- not interfere with the Service, probe its security, or overload it with automated requests;
- not resell or give access to the Service to third parties as a commercial service without the Provider's written consent.

AI results are drafts. A student or anyone working under academic rules must follow their institution's rules, review the text, check facts and sources, and present only work they can stand behind as their own.

The **Sign PDF** feature places an image of a signature on a page. It is not an electronic digital signature (ERI/EDS) and has no legal effect of one.

## 11. AI-generated content

AI results are made automatically and may contain mistakes, outdated facts or invented references. The User checks every result before using it and is responsible for how it is used.

The Provider does not promise that a result is original, unique, or will pass any plagiarism or AI-detection check.

To review quality and prevent misuse, the Provider keeps AI requests, outlines and results for 30 days, then deletes them. Details are in the [Privacy policy](/en/legal/privacy).

Uploaded files and results are deleted 24 hours after upload or creation. The User downloads results within that time and keeps their own copies of important files.
