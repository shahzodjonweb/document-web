This policy is part of the [Public offer](/en/legal/offer) of "ARCANIUM TECHNOLOGIES" MCHJ (ARCANIUM TECHNOLOGIES LLC), STIR 313458514.

## 1. Scope

This policy covers payments for the Plus and Premium plans, each bought for a 30-day period by bank-card transfer.

Plus months earned through invite rewards are given at no charge. They are not a payment, so they are not refunded or exchanged for money.

## 2. No auto-renewal

Plans do not renew automatically, and we never charge your card on our own. There is nothing to cancel: a plan simply ends after 30 days, and your account returns to Free unless you pay again.

## 3. When you get a full refund

We refund the full amount in each of these cases:

- **Within 3 days of activation, with no task run.** You ask within 3 days of the moment your paid plan was switched on, and you have not run any task (a file tool, an AI document, slides, a CV or an AI edit) on that plan.
- **Duplicate payment.** You paid twice for the same period by mistake. We refund the extra payment.
- **Plan never activated.** You paid, but the plan was never switched on (for example, the payment could not be matched or we declined it).

## 4. When a refund is not available

We do not refund a payment:

- if more than 3 days have passed since activation; or
- if at least one task was run on the paid plan.

We do not refund part of a period. When a period ends, unused weekly limits are not carried over or paid back.

> LAWYER: Check the Consumer Protection Law on refunds for unused paid services and partial periods, and adjust this section if a statutory right applies.

## 5. How to ask for a refund

Write to support at {{support.url}} or to {{company.email}} and include:

- the payment receipt;
- the account you paid for (Telegram username, email or the name shown in the app);
- the payment date and amount.

We reply and tell you our decision. If we need more details, we ask for them.

> LAWYER: Confirm whether a written request in a set form is required and the statutory deadline for our answer.

## 6. Method and timing

Refunds go back to the same card the payment came from, within 10 working days after we approve the refund. Your bank may need extra time to show the money on your account.

We do not refund in cash or to another card or person.

## 7. What happens to your plan

When a refund for an active plan is approved, the paid plan ends and your account returns to Free. For a duplicate payment, the plan you paid for once stays active.

## 8. Receipts

We keep payment receipts for 90 days after our decision on the payment, then delete them. See the [Privacy policy](/en/legal/privacy).
