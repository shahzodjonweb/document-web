1.Parties

This public offer is made by “ARCANIUM TECHNOLOGIES” MCHJ (STIR 313458514), Toshkent sh., Yunusobod t. (the Seller), to any person who uses DocMaster (the User).

2.Subject

The Seller gives the User access to the DocMaster services: PDF file tools, the PDF editor and AI documents, slides and CVs, on the website docmaster.live and in the DocMaster Telegram bot.

3.Acceptance

The User accepts this offer by signing in to DocMaster or by paying for a plan. From that moment the contract is concluded on the terms below.

Lawyer to cite the Civil Code articles on public offer and acceptance.

4.Plans and prices

Plans and prices are listed on the Pricing page in so‘m: Free (0 so‘m), Plus (20 000 so‘m for 30 days) and Premium (60 000 so‘m for 30 days). A temporary discount shows its end date.

5.Payment and activation

The User pays by card transfer and uploads the receipt. The plan starts when the Seller approves the receipt and lasts 30 days. Plans don’t renew automatically.

6.Limits and acceptable use

Weekly limits depend on the plan. Files can be up to 200 MB and 1000 pages per task on the web and 20 MB per file in the bot.

7.AI content

AI results can contain mistakes. The User checks them before use. AI requests and results are kept for 30 days for quality review.

8.Refunds

Refunds follow the Refund policy and go back to the card used for payment.

9.Liability and disputes

The laws of the Republic of Uzbekistan apply. Disputes are settled by negotiation first, then in court.

10.Language

This offer is published in Uzbek, Russian and English. If the texts differ, the Uzbek text prevails.
