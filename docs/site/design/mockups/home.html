Help centre
Is DocMaster free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 5 AI documents and 15 file tasks a week, and 3 AI edits per file. Plus and Premium raise the limits.

Is it safe to upload my files?

Uploaded files and results are deleted after 24 hours. There are no analytics or ad trackers. AI requests are kept for 30 days for quality review. The privacy policy has the details.

Which languages does it work in?

The site and the bot work in Uzbek, Russian and English. AI documents, slides and CVs can be written in any of the three.

Should I use the bot or the website?

Either: it is the same account and plan. The bot takes files up to 20 MB. On the web a task can be up to 200 MB and 1000 pages.

How do I pay?

By card transfer from Uzcard, Humo, Visa or Mastercard. Upload the receipt in the app and the plan starts once it is approved.

Can I change an AI document after it is made?

Yes, with AI edits: 3 per file on Free, 10 on Plus, unlimited on Premium.
