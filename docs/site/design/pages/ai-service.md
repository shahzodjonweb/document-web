# Template: ai-service (`/{locale}/ai/{documents|slides|cv}`)

Mockup: `mockups/ai-service.html` (slides variant). Data: `plans`; galleries need real
exports from the product.

## Shared skeleton

| Slot | documents | slides | cv |
|---|---|---|---|
| hero visual | document page fan (cover + inner page, folded corners) | two 16:9 slides + "Slide 4 of 18" chip | CV page + template picker strip |
| main CTA | "Create a document" | "Create a presentation" | "Build a CV" |
| hero chips | Up to 35 pages · uz/ru/en · PDF | Up to 60 slides · 110 designs · Editable .pptx | 50 templates · 3 ways to start · uz/ru/en |
| gallery | `examples` (real covers/pages; placeholder until owner supplies) | `designs` (real picker thumbnails, 12 shown, "See all 110" in app) | `templates` (real thumbnails, 12 shown) |

## Layout

1440: hero 50/50, gallery 6 columns, splits 2 columns. 768: hero stacks (visual under copy,
max 560 px), gallery 4 columns. 390: CTAs full width, gallery 2 columns and 8 items,
start-mode tiles stack.

## Data bindings

- `plans`: AI docs per week, edits per file, prices in tile labels.
- Galleries: static exported images committed under `public/site/{designs,cv,examples}/`
  (WebP + 2x), generated by the existing deck/CV preview tools; names come from the design
  catalog so they localize.
- Counts (35 pages, 60 slides, 110 designs, 50 templates) are product constants; read them
  from config where available so the pages follow product changes.
